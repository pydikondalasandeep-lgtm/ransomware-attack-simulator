STAGE 04

🔄 Persistence — Staying Inside

Simple meaning: Persistence means an attacker attempts to maintain access to an environment after the original entry point has been discovered or removed.

In this project, persistence is represented safely. No real persistence mechanism is created.

🎬 MedCore — Scene 1

10:22 AM

🖥️ Chakra notices something unusual

Chakra, the workstation owner, notices that the computer is behaving differently from normal.

"Sandeep, something feels wrong with this system."
10:24 AM

🚨 Sandeep receives an alert

Sandeep, the SOC analyst, sees suspicious activity associated with Chakra's workstation.

"Don't disconnect anything yet. I'm checking the alert."
10:26 AM

🛠️ Sarvan joins the investigation

Sarvan, the system administrator, begins checking the affected workstation and account activity.

"I'll verify the workstation and account status."

🧠 What is happening here?

The attacker is represented as attempting to maintain a foothold.

The defenders are looking for signs that access may still exist.

This is the concept of Persistence.

🛡️ Defender Response

✓ Review suspicious account activity
✓ Check affected systems
✓ Investigate unusual authentication events
✓ Remove or disable confirmed compromised access
STAGE 05

🔎 Discovery — Understanding the Environment

Simple meaning: Discovery means learning about the environment.

An attacker may try to understand what systems, users, servers and important resources exist.

Think of it as: "What is inside this organization?"

🎬 MedCore — Scene 2

10:31 AM

🌐 Mani checks the network

Mani, the network analyst, reviews the network activity surrounding the affected workstation.

"There is unusual communication coming from Chakra's system."
10:34 AM

🔍 Sandeep connects the alerts

Sandeep compares the endpoint alert with authentication and network information.

"The events are connected. We may be looking at one incident."
10:37 AM

🚨 Siri takes command

Siri, the incident-response lead, coordinates the investigation and prepares the team for possible containment.

"Keep monitoring. Prepare containment procedures."

🧠 What is Discovery?

The attacker is represented as trying to understand the environment before moving further.

For defenders, unusual discovery activity can be an important warning sign.

The earlier the team understands what is happening, the better the chance of stopping the attack.

🛡️ Defender Response

✓ Monitor unusual network behaviour
✓ Maintain an accurate asset inventory
✓ Investigate unexpected account activity
✓ Correlate endpoint, identity and network alerts
