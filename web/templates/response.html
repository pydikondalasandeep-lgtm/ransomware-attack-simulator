🧠 What Did We Learn?

A ransomware incident is not just about the final ransom screen.

It is a chain of events:

Phishing → Initial Access → Execution → Persistence → Discovery → Impact → Response → Recovery

Detecting the attack earlier can give defenders more opportunities to stop the chain before serious impact occurs.
