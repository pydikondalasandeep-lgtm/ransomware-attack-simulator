What is ransomware?

Ransomware is malicious software designed to deny access to data or systems, commonly by encrypting files, and attackers may demand payment for recovery.

Why is this dangerous?

Organizations can lose access to important information and services. In healthcare, disruption can also affect normal operations.

Why does the attacker ask for money?

The attacker attempts to use the loss of availability as pressure on the organization to make a ransom payment.

Is paying the ransom guaranteed to work?

No. Payment does not guarantee recovery and does not remove the underlying security risks.

🛡 What Should Defenders Do?

  • Isolate affected systems from the network.
  • Preserve evidence and investigate the attack.
  • Determine how the attacker entered the environment.
  • Check whether other systems are affected.
  • Use clean backups and recovery procedures where available.
  • Document the incident and improve defenses.
