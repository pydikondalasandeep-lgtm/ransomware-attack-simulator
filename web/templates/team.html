Meet the MedCore Team

Before the incident begins, meet the six people involved in our fictional healthcare environment. Each person has a different responsibility during the simulated ransomware incident.

S
Sahithi
Hospital Employee / First Victim
Sahithi receives the suspicious message that begins the simulated attack scenario. Her actions help demonstrate how phishing can become the starting point of a ransomware incident.
C
Chakra
Workstation Owner
Chakra is responsible for the workstation that later becomes the focus of the simulated investigation. Unusual behaviour on this system becomes an important warning sign.
S
Sandeep
SOC Security Analyst
Sandeep monitors security alerts, connects suspicious events and helps identify the ransomware attack chain.
S
Sarvan
System Administrator
Sarvan investigates affected systems and helps verify workstation and account activity during the incident.
M
Mani
Network Analyst
Mani observes simulated network activity and investigates unusual communication related to the incident.
S
Siri
Incident Response Lead
Siri coordinates the response team and helps decide how the organization should contain and respond to the simulated ransomware incident.
